## 🚀 Overview

This repository is dedicated to organizing and optimizing daily tax and accounting operations, particularly focusing on:
* **Tax Calculation & Validation:** Managing routine procedures for companies under **Simples Nacional** and **Lucro Presumido** tax regimes, including federal and municipal tax computations (PIS, COFINS, CSRF, IRRF, INSS, ISS).
* **Excel Automation & Workflows:** Utilizing advanced spreadsheets for reconciliations, XML invoice analysis, and report validation from systems like **Sistema Domínio** and government portals (**e-CAC, DCTFWeb, EFD, EFD-Reinf**).
* **Task Organization:** Structuring schedules and workflows to ensure legal compliance and deadline adherence without errors.

---

## 📂 Repository Structure & Featured Guides

### 1. Municipal Invoice Cancellation Guide (`/guides/`)
* **Description:** Explanatory spreadsheet (with up-to-date notes) on municipal invoice cancellation procedures across different city hall portals. 
* **Purpose:** Serves as a practical guide for professionals who need to understand, navigate, and execute invoice cancellations safely across various municipal systems.

### 2. General Tax Activities & Workflow Tracker (`/spreadsheets/`)
* **Description:** General tax and workflow tracker for task description, operational control, and general task organization.
* **Purpose:** Optimizes daily operational tasks, document management, and fiscal scheduling.

### 3. Tax Manual & Compliance Documentation (`/manual/`)
* **Description:** Detailed reference manuals covering core tax legislation, accessory obligations (**DCTFWeb, EFD-Reinf R-2099/R-4099**), and early preparations for the **Tax Reform (IBS and CBS)**.
